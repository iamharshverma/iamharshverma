Extended biography — 250 words
Harsh Verma is a Principal Software Engineer at Palo Alto Networks, working at the intersection of AI, cybersecurity and agentic systems. Based in the San Francisco Bay Area, he brings an engineering perspective to the design of intelligent systems that must remain reliable, secure and accountable in enterprise environments.

His technical interests include machine learning, distributed systems, autonomous workflows and enterprise AI architecture. His professional background includes computer vision work at Hypergiant Sensory Science and real-time object tracking and spatial analytics at Worlds Enterprises. Public projects on his portfolio explore concurrent data structures, distributed data processing and information retrieval.

Harsh studied Computer Science and Data Science at The University of Texas at Dallas and pursued executive study in leadership and entrepreneurship at Stanford Graduate School of Business. His earlier education includes a B.Tech in Computer Science and Engineering at Uttarakhand Technical University.

Beyond his engineering role, Harsh contributes to Forbes Technology Council and speaks about agentic AI, enterprise security and responsible AI adoption. His portfolio includes keynote recordings, public research references, granted patents and pending inventions, with statuses distinguished rather than treated as interchangeable achievements. He also participates in mentorship and developer communities, including UC Berkeley SkyDeck and Google Developer Experts.

His forthcoming books, Beyond AI Engineering and AI vs. AI, examine the changing role of engineers and the security implications of autonomous intelligence. Both are planned for January 2027. His personal writing emphasizes human judgment, evidence-based trust, permission boundaries and the practical responsibilities of building systems that can act.
