# Harsh Verma — Advanced Personal Portfolio

A premium, interactive portfolio positioning Harsh as a Principal AI Engineer, researcher, advisor and speaker, reusing all content from harshverma.me.

## Visual direction
- Dark "Noir & Gold"-inspired theme: near-black canvas, warm gold accents, subtle electric-cyan for AI motifs.
- Typography: Instrument Serif (headlines, editorial gravitas) + Work Sans (body), JetBrains Mono for small data labels.
- Motion: smooth scroll reveals, animated stat counters, magnetic buttons, cursor glow, subtle neural-network particle field in the hero.
- Light/dark toggle.

## Pages (each with its own SEO title/description)
1. **Home** — hero with portrait, rotating role titles (Principal AI Engineer, Speaker, Investor, Author, Advisor), animated stats (12+ yrs, 27+ papers, 6 patents, 44+ press), featured logos marquee (Palo Alto Networks, Forbes, IEEE, Stanford, UC Berkeley SkyDeck), highlights teasers.
2. **About** — full biography, the "three P's" philosophy, signature, interactive career + education timeline (Palo Alto Networks, Stanford GSB, UT Dallas, UTU), fellowships and memberships grid.
3. **Research** — filterable list of papers and patents (by year/topic), with citation links to Google Scholar.
4. **Speaking & Events** — keynotes, panels, judging; photo gallery with lightbox; "Book a keynote" CTA.
5. **Press & Media** — 44+ features as a searchable wall of publication cards.
6. **Advisory & Mentoring** — SkyDeck, Forbes Council, startup advisory, focus areas.
7. **Contact** — inquiry form (keynote / advisory / media / other), email, social links, verified profiles list.

## Interactive features
- **"Ask Harsh's AI"** chat assistant trained on his bio, papers and talks (answers visitor questions).
- Command palette (Cmd+K) to jump to any section, paper or talk.
- Animated skills/expertise constellation (Agentic AI, AI Security, ML, NLP, CV).
- Interactive world map of talks and press locations.
- Downloadable press kit / one-page bio.
- Testimonials carousel.

## Optional (needs Lovable Cloud)
